About the series

In 2038 the city of Thessaloniki still meets the water the way it always has. Above that ordinary light, a laboratory is pursuing work that refuses a single name — part physics, part memory, part something the instruments were not built to expect.

SINGULAB is that story, told at a measured pace. Conversations carry the weight. Discoveries arrive without fanfare, and they stay in the room afterward. The tone is closer to a starship briefing than a chase: precise, human, and unwilling to look away.

It is a series for adults. Six presences share the facility. What they are building, and what is gathering around them, is the question the episodes keep.

The crew

A short manifest. No one here is background.

01

William

Human scientist

Lead of the laboratory. He keeps every question tied to a person who can answer for it.

02

T'Ara

Non-human

Pointed ears, a white uniform traced in cyan. She is already on the next thought, and shares only what the moment can hold.

03

Talos

Android

Smooth as white marble, with a black visor crossed by a single cyan line. Stillness, in him, is a kind of attention.

04

Omnia

Being of light

Luminous, translucent blue — a constellation willing to hold a shape. When she enters, the air looks briefly deeper.

05

Archon

Drone

A white sphere, floating. It watches, carries, and acts with almost no ceremony.

06

Nexus

Facility computer

Heard, not seen. A robotic voice that belongs to the building: doors, warnings, and the occasional fact no one requested.
